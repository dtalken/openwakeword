┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 COMPLETE WORKFLOW                                                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Setup (ONE TIME - fixes architecture issues):
   bash fix_architecture.sh

2. ALWAYS activate virtual environment before working:
   source venv/bin/activate
   
   (You'll see "(venv)" in your prompt)

3. Check Dataset:
   python3 check_dataset.py

4. Normalize Audio:
   python3 normalize_audio.py

5. Train Model:
   bash train.sh
   
   OR manually:
   python3 -m openwakeword.train --config train_config.yaml

6. Test Model:
   python3 test_model.py dataset/positive/sample.wav

7. Deploy:
   Copy models/hey_mel.tflite to your Flutter app

8. When done:
   deactivate
